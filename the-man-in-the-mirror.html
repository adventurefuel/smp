The Heart Of The Matter · No. 01 · Identity

The Man In The Mirror

After years of putting everyone first, this one is for you.

By Terrence HopsonCEO & Founder, Swurl Kurl Studios · 4 min read

I want to start with a question. Answer it honestly, just for yourself. Nobody is grading this one.

01When was the last time you really saw yourself?

Not a glance. Not a quick check on your way to work, or the school pickup, or Sunday service. I mean a real look, the kind you'd give someone you care about.

For a lot of men, the honest answer is a long time ago. Life got loud. There was always somewhere to be and someone who needed something, and the mirror slowly turned into a tool. Shave, brush, check the collar, go.

I've spent thirty years behind the chair, and the mirror is where a lot of the real conversations start. A man sits down, looks up, and goes quiet for a second. Then he says something like, “I don't really recognize myself lately.” He says it softly, almost like an apology. It's one of the most honest things a man can say, and it deserves more than a shrug.

02You lead. You provide. You show up.

That's the job, and you've been doing it. You show up for your family before the sun does. You show up for your work, your friends, your church, your block, the people who call you when something breaks. You carry more than most people ever see, and you almost never say how heavy it is.

There's real honor in that. I'm not here to tell you to carry less.

But somewhere in all that carrying, a lot of men quietly move themselves to the bottom of the list. Not out of weakness. Out of love. There was always someone who needed it more, so your comfort, your health, your own reflection waited. And waited. Then the years went by.

The man who carries a lot deserves to feel like himself again.02 · You lead. You provide. You show up.

03You deserve to feel good about yourself, too.

Confidence is personal. It looks different on every man. For some it's a fresh cut and a sharp shirt. For some it's a quiet morning before the house wakes up. For some it's a hairline they've been missing for a long time.

None of that is vanity. How you feel about the man in the mirror shapes how you walk into a room, how you talk to the people you love, and how much of yourself you bring to the day. When you feel good about you, everyone you carry feels it.

You don't have to earn this by doing one more thing for somebody else first.

So put yourself back on the list. Not above everyone. Just back on it.

Confidence is personal. And it matters.03 · You deserve to feel good about you, too.

04See yourself again. Explore SMP.

This is where I tell you what I do, because it's what I know best.

Scalp micropigmentation, or SMP, is a way to rebuild the look of a closely shaved head of hair, or a hairline that has stepped back over the years. Pigment is placed in the scalp, designed with you and around your features, so the result looks like it has always belonged to you. It's not a tattoo of a line, and it isn't about looking twenty-five again. It's about looking like you, on your best day.

It isn't the answer for everyone, and it isn't the only way to feel like yourself. But if the thought keeps coming back when you're standing at that mirror, maybe it's worth a conversation. No pressure. Just an honest talk about what you want to see when you look up.

SMP helps you see yourself with renewed confidence.04 · See yourself again.

One last thingSay good morning to him.

Tomorrow morning, before the day starts asking for you, look up. Take a real look. Say good morning to the man you see. He has carried a lot, and he's still here.

Then ask him what he needs.

See yourself again.

Terrence Hopson
CEO & Founder, Swurl Kurl Studios
