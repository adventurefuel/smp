Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This explains what Swurl Kurl Studios ("the studio," "we") collects when you use this website, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • When you request a consultation, booking or training information: your name, mobile number, email address (if you give one), what you would like to improve, how and when you prefer to be contacted, a preferred appointment date or time, how you heard about us, the barbershop that referred you (optional), and anything you choose to tell us.
  • When you subscribe to our blog: your email address and, if you give it, your first name.
  • When you visit the site: basic information such as the pages viewed, your device and browser type, and the website or ad that brought you here.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request by phone, text message or email, and to arrange your consultation or appointment.
  • To keep a record of your requests, conversations and appointments so the studio can follow up with you.
  • To send blog updates to people who subscribed.
  • To understand how people find and use the site and to measure our advertising.

Text messages and email

By submitting a request form you agree that the studio may contact you about your request by phone, text message or email using the details you gave. Message and data rates may apply. To stop contact, tell us in your reply or through the form on this site.

Tools and service providers

We use trusted software providers to run the site, store requests, and manage scheduling and follow-up. This includes website hosting, a database, and software that helps the studio organize requests and prepare follow-up messages, which may include automated (AI-assisted) tools. These providers can see the contact and request information described above so they can do that work. We do not put payment card details in these tools. Messages to you are reviewed and sent by the studio.

Maps

The map on our site only loads if you press "View Live Map." When you do, it is served by Google Maps, which may collect information under its own privacy policy. The directions and call buttons simply open your maps app or phone.

Advertising and analytics

We may use advertising and analytics tools such as the Meta Pixel and a Google tag. They may use cookies or similar technology to measure visits and show you our ads on other sites. We use them to learn that a page was viewed or a request form was submitted. We do not send them your name, phone number, email address or what you wrote in a form. You can limit this through your browser's cookie settings and the ad settings in your Meta and Google accounts.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by telling us through the request form on this site or when we reach out. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
