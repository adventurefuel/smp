Touch-Ups, Explained

Questions

SMP FAQ

Everything you're wondering before you book your consultation.

Will My SMP Look Natural?

That's the goal, and it's the standard every session is built around. SMP is applied to replicate the look of closely shaved, natural hair follicles — not a tattoo, and not an obvious hairline. Results vary from person to person, which is why we talk through your skin, your hair and the look you want during your consultation.

How Many Sessions Will I Need?

Most clients complete their results in two sessions, though the exact number can vary depending on the size and location of the area being treated. Your consultation will lay out a plan built around your specific hair loss pattern.

Can SMP Cover Scars?

Often it can help soften how a scar looks, but scar tissue behaves differently from healthy scalp skin. It can hold pigment less predictably, so results vary from one scar to the next. Realistically, that can mean more than one session with healing time in between, and sometimes a touch-up sooner than you'd need elsewhere. Some scars may not be a good fit at all. The type of scar, how old it is and how well it has healed all matter, so we look at it in person during your free consultation and talk through what to expect, including whether SMP is the right choice for you. That goes for transplant scars, injury scars and surgical scars alike.

Do You Offer SMP For Women?

Yes, we offer SMP for women. Women's goals are often different, such as the look of more density through thinning areas, a widening part, or less contrast between hair and scalp, so your plan is built around you. SMP creates the appearance of fuller coverage; it doesn't regrow hair. Let's talk through your goals in a free consultation.

Does SMP Hurt?

Comfort varies from person to person, and from one area of the scalp to another. Many clients find it manageable, and some areas, like the hairline and temples, can feel more sensitive. Numbing spray and cream are available, and if you're more tender-headed, tell us and we'll plan your session around your comfort. Mild redness or tenderness afterward is common.

How Long Does SMP Last?

SMP is long-lasting, not forever. The pigment stays in the skin, but it can soften or lighten gradually over the years, often noticeably after several years, depending on your skin, sun exposure, aftercare and how your body holds pigment. Many clients book a touch-up down the road to refresh density and edges. Day to day, regular hair and scalp care products are all it takes. See "Touch-Ups, Explained" above for how that works.

How Do You Choose My Hairline?

Your hairline is never assumed — it's chosen together, through consultation, around a natural line that fits your features and the look you want. And "natural" means different things to different men: plenty of our clients, especially those 50 and up, aren't looking to look 25 again. They want a hairline that looks like it's always belonged to them, and that's exactly what we build toward.

How Do I Know If I'm A Good Candidate?

There's no need to wait until hair loss is advanced. As soon as you start noticing the progression — a thinning crown, a receding hairline, wherever it shows up for you — that's the right time to start the conversation and understand your options.
