Questions

SMP FAQ

Everything you're wondering before you book your consultation.

Will My SMP Look Natural?

Yes. Natural results are the entire point of the process. SMP is applied to replicate the look of closely shaved, natural hair follicles — not a tattoo, and not an obvious hairline. That's the standard every session is built around.

How Many Sessions Will I Need?

Most clients complete their results in two sessions, though the exact number can vary depending on the size and location of the area being treated. Your consultation will lay out a plan built around your specific hair loss pattern.

Does SMP Hurt?

No. SMP is a far more comfortable process than most people expect. If you're more tender-headed or simply want extra comfort during your session, numbing spray and cream are available.

How Long Does SMP Last?

SMP is permanent. Once it's done, it's done — regular daily hair and scalp care products are all it takes to keep your results looking their best.

How Do You Choose My Hairline?

Your hairline is never assumed — it's chosen together, through consultation, around a natural line that fits your features and the look you want. And "natural" means different things to different men: plenty of our clients, especially those 50 and up, aren't looking to look 25 again. They want a hairline that looks like it's always belonged to them, and that's exactly what we build toward.

How Do I Know If I'm A Good Candidate?

There's no need to wait until hair loss is advanced. As soon as you start noticing the progression — a thinning crown, a receding hairline, wherever it shows up for you — that's the right time to start the conversation and understand your options.
